Afferent arteriole — myogenic response

Normal vascular tone

As pressure rises, the afferent arteriole constricts to limit increases in blood flow.

Key mechanisms

Myogenic response: Increased transmural pressure stretches vascular smooth muscle → contraction → increased afferent resistance.

Tubuloglomerular feedback: Increased NaCl delivery to the macula densa promotes afferent arteriolar constriction through ATP/adenosine signalling.

Important: At very low pressures, vasodilation cannot fully maintain RBF or GFR; above the upper limit, autoregulation becomes less effective.
